# The default query set. One per line; lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# Chosen to span the thing that most changes latency: whether the answer needs
# research at all. A sweep over only evergreen questions will make search look
# free, and a sweep over only breaking news will make it look ruinous.

# Evergreen - answerable from what the model already knows.
why do tides happen
how does a heat pump work
what actually causes jet lag

# Moving targets - a searched arm should have to work for these.
what changed in the news today
what happened in the markets this week

# Narrow and factual - short answers, where fixed overhead dominates.
who wrote the first computer program
